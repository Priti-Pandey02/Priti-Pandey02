## About Me

I am **Priti Kumari Pandey**, a B.Tech CSE-AIML student at **Parul University**, expected to graduate in 2029.

I am passionate about **software development, artificial intelligence, web technologies, and problem solving**. I enjoy turning ideas into practical projects while continuously strengthening my programming and development fundamentals.

My current technical foundation includes **Java, JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3, C, C++**, responsive web development, Git/GitHub, Data Structures & Algorithms, and Object-Oriented Programming.

I have also participated in hackathons and technical activities, including being a **1X finalist in PU CODE 3.0 Hackathon**, where my team worked on an AI-based drug prediction project. I have also been shortlisted for the **Women Who Master Hackathon Zonal Round**.

> **Currently focused on:** building stronger software engineering fundamentals, exploring AI/ML, developing real-world web applications, and improving problem-solving skills.
